## Why schedules matter

Missing a dose can reset immunity windows. A structured schedule keeps DHPP, rabies, and lifestyle vaccines on track.

## Puppy basics

Most puppies start a series between 6–8 weeks, with boosters every 3–4 weeks until 16 weeks, then rabies per local law and vet advice.

## Adult dogs

Annual or triennial boosters depend on vaccine type and your vet’s protocol. Pawfect adapts reminders to the plan you confirm with your clinic.
